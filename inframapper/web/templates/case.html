How fidelity and confidence are scored — heuristic, explainable, not probabilities

Confidence — how strongly the evidence supports that this entity is related to the investigation. Each relationship = observation strength (a served certificate or DNS resolution is direct; a string in a binary or a VT comment is not) × source reliability, combined across independent sources, × freshness, × conflicts (analyst false positive, reports outside the usage window, domain resolves elsewhere now). An entity's confidence follows the strongest chain from the seed; passing through a shared artefact costs its specificity (HTML body ≫ page title), shared hosting passes almost nothing. One source alone is capped at 80.

Fidelity — how useful the entity is for this investigation: 30% indicator specificity + 25% confidence + 20% context (hops from the seed, cluster membership) + 15% threat signal (triage priority, VT detections) + 10% freshness, × false-positive risk (warninglists, Tor, shared hosting…), × a relevance gate below confidence 40.

Bands: high ≥ 75 · medium ≥ 50 · low ≥ 25 · very low. n/a = insufficient evidence (no path to the seed). These are heuristic rankings, not calibrated probabilities: 80 does not mean “80% likely”. Open any row to see every factor and its points.
